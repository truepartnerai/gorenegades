## Founder post

I'm the worst at consistency. I have never once woken up and thought, "What should I be consistent about today?" So about a year ago I asked my high school son to set up a simple automation that emails my whole database twice a week. It took him ten minutes, and my part since then is about five minutes a week to drop in the next email.

Are the emails great? Not always. Some are three lines, and once or twice the same email went out two weeks in a row. Nobody complained. What people did do was call, reply, and tell me "I've been getting your emails…", and about 90% of the time they called about something I had never emailed about. The consistency paid, not the content.

Whole story: https://gorenegades.com/blog/one-little-automation-five-minutes-a-week/
