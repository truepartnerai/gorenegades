Why I built this

Mortgage people are taught mortgages. They're rarely taught marketing.

I've spent more than two decades in this industry, including years teaching and coaching mortgage professionals. I kept seeing the same gap: good originators knew how to take care of a borrower once the opportunity showed up, but many had never been taught how to systematically create that opportunity in the first place.

That's why Renegade Academy is built around implementation. We'll talk strategy, but then we're going to open the software, build the campaign, wire up the follow-up, look at the numbers, and make it usable in the real world. The goal is not to make you dependent on Renegade. It's to make you better at creating and managing your own demand.
